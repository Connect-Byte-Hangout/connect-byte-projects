About the project

A portable focus timer that combines an ESP32-C3 Super Mini, an OLED display, two buttons, and a buzzer to organize work and break cycles.

The project was developed for Connect Byte's November workshop and introduces state-based programming, button input, non-blocking timing, and visual and audible feedback.

How it works

  • The display shows the remaining time, current cycle, and a progress bar.
  • B2 starts, pauses, and resumes the timer.
  • B1 resets the cycle.
  • While idle, hold B1 for 1 second to adjust work and break durations.
  • While idle, hold B2 for 2 seconds to enable or disable sound.
  • At the end of each stage, choose whether to start another focus session or a break.

Firmware

The firmware uses the Arduino Framework with PlatformIO and is available in firmware/. It targets the ESP32-C3 Super Mini and a 128 x 32 SSD1306 OLED display at I2C address 0x3C.

Open the firmware/ folder in VS Code with PlatformIO to build it. See the repository's PlatformIO guide for setup instructions.

🔌 Assembly Guide

Before you begin

Keep power disconnected while wiring. Verify the battery's positive and negative terminals before connecting it, and confirm every connection against the pin table below.

Signal wiring

ComponentConnection
OLED SDAGPIO 4
OLED SCLGPIO 5
OLED VCCCompatible supply
OLED GNDGND
Button B1GPIO 0 and GND
Button B2GPIO 1 and GND
Buzzer positiveGPIO 7
Buzzer negativeGND

The buttons use INPUT_PULLUP, so they are active-low and do not need external pull-up resistors.

Power

Wire the battery, protected charger, and power switch as shown in the diagram while following the specifications of the components in your kit. Do not program or power the board until continuity, voltage, and polarity have been checked.

Programming

  1. Install VS Code and PlatformIO using the English guide.
  2. Open the firmware/ folder as a PlatformIO project.
  3. Connect the ESP32-C3 with a USB data cable.
  4. Build and upload the firmware.
  5. Open the serial monitor at 115200 to follow startup.

Materials

🛠️ Bill of Materials

  • 1 ESP32-C3 Super Mini board
  • 1 128 x 32 SSD1306 I2C OLED display
  • 2 momentary push buttons
  • 1 passive buzzer
  • 1 rechargeable battery compatible with the circuit
  • 1 protected USB-C charger module compatible with the battery
  • 1 on/off switch
  • Hookup wire
  • 1 USB data cable for programming
Verify battery and charger model, voltage, and polarity before assembly. Never connect a battery with reversed polarity.
