## 🛠️ Bill of Materials
To build this project, you will need the following components:

* **1x ESP32 Development Board**
* **1x Buzzer**.
* **3x LED:** To change the color, make sure to verify the correct resistor value.
* **3x Resistor:** Recommended values between 220Ω and 470Ω, depending on the LEDs used.
* **1x Perfboard (Prototype Board)**
* **4x Large Push Buttons**
* **Jumper Wires:** For the connections, don't worry about matching the exact colors in the diagram. Just follow the standard practice: use darker wires for negative and lighter wires for positive.
